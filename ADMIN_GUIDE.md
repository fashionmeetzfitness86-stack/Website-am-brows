# 👑 Ashley M. Brows — Admin Dashboard Guide

Welcome to the Ashley M. Brows Studio Management Portal. This dashboard allows you to manage consultation requests, communicate with clients, and keep your schedule organized.

---

## 🔐 1. Logging In
1. Navigate to: `https://ashleymbrows.com/login` (or your chosen domain).
2. Enter your authorized email address and password.
3. Upon successful login, you will be redirected to your private Admin Dashboard (`/admin`).
4. **Note:** Unauthorized users or users without explicit staff/admin permission will be shown an "Access Denied" screen and will not be able to enter the dashboard.

## 📥 2. Managing Consultation Requests
The **Consultation Requests** tab shows all client submissions. 
- You will see the **Requested** date and time.
- The **Confirmed** date and time will be blank or marked "Pending Review" until you explicitly confirm the appointment.
- **Search:** Use the search bar to find specific clients by name, email, or requested date.
- **Filter:** Filter by status (e.g., "New Request", "Confirmed", "Cancelled") using the buttons at the top of the table.

## ✅ 3. Confirming Consultations
To review and confirm a booking:
1. Click the **Manage** button on any row to open the detailed panel.
2. Review the client's information, intake answers, and uploaded photos (if any).
3. **If you accept the requested time:**
   - Click the green **✓ Approve Requested Time** button.
   - This automatically copies the requested date/time to the confirmed date/time and updates the status to **Confirmed**.
4. **If you need to change the time:**
   - Use the **Set Custom Confirmed Time** section to pick a new date and time.
   - Click **Save Confirmed Time**.

## ✉️ 4. Sending Confirmation Emails
Once a date/time is confirmed, you must notify the client:
1. Inside the Manage panel, scroll down to the **Confirmation Email** section.
2. Click **Send Confirmation** (if the requested time was approved) OR click **Send Reschedule** (if you modified the time).
3. The system will automatically send a beautifully branded HTML email to the client with the *Confirmed* date and time.
4. A green checkmark will appear indicating when the email was sent (`✓ Email sent [Date]`).

## 📝 5. Admin Notes
- You can leave private notes on any booking in the **Admin Notes** section.
- These notes are visible *only* to you and other staff members. They will not be seen by the client.
- Use this for tracking client preparation instructions, follow-up reminders, or internal comments.

## 🔄 6. Statuses
You can manually update the status of a request via the dropdown in the Manage panel. Available statuses:
- **New Request:** A brand new, untouched submission.
- **Under Review:** You are currently looking at it or waiting on client correspondence.
- **Confirmed:** You have officially scheduled the consultation.
- **Rescheduled:** The appointment was moved.
- **Completed:** The consultation happened successfully.
- **Cancelled:** The consultation will not proceed.
- **No Show:** The client failed to attend the consultation.

## 🚨 7. Logging Out
- Always log out of the dashboard if using a shared or public computer.
- Click the **Sign Out** button in the bottom left of the sidebar to securely end your session.
